Pathway

This sets which factors are scored and what your maximum is.

Occupation

Max 19 points

Type a job title. Your NOC code sets both your TEER level and your occupational category, so this one answer scores two factors.

Compensation

Max 15 points

The hourly wage your employer states on the job offer, before deductions.

Experience in the Offer Position

Max 18 points

Months worked in the exact position named on your job offer.

Ontario scores this factor and the next one, then keeps whichever is higher. They are never added together, so the breakdown shows a single experience line.

Experience Anywhere in Ontario

Max 12 points

Any Ontario work experience, counted separately from the offer position above.

Medical Practice in Ontario

Max 18 points

Cumulative months of practice. It does not need to be continuous.

Canadian Tax History

Max 8 points

Your highest single year of income in the last five tax years, as assessed by the CRA.

Status in Canada

Max 10 points

Your current valid immigration status, if any.

Highest Credential

Max 10 points

Only your single highest completed credential counts.

Canadian Credentials

Max 10 points

Credentials earned at a Canadian institution, scored on top of your highest credential above.

Language Level

Max 15 points

Use your stronger official language, then take the lowest of your four abilities on that test: listening, reading, writing, speaking.

Official Languages

Max 10 points

Scored on top of your CLB level above.

Work Location

Max 15 points

Use the work location on the job offer, not your home address. Physicians: use the practice address registered to your OHIP billing number.

Before you rely on this score

Ontario published this scoring grid on 20 July 2026 and it may change. No invitation cut-off score has been announced for the Workforce Priority Stream, so a high score here does not mean you will be invited to apply.

This calculator estimates points only. It does not assess your eligibility or check the stream’s minimum requirements for work experience, language and education, all of which must be met before any points count. It is not immigration advice and does not create a consultant client relationship.
